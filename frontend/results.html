Monosodium Glutamate

Flavor enhancer

check_circleIdentified
help_outline

What is it?

A savory seasoning derived from glutamic acid, an amino acid naturally found in foods like tomatoes, aged cheese, and mushrooms.

restaurant

Why is it used?

Used to bring out savory umami flavors in snack seasonings and prepared foods.

info

What should I know?

Widely used in everyday foods around the world. Standard food regulatory guidelines classify it as safe for general consumption.
