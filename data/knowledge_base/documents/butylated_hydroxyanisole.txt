Ingredient:
Butylated Hydroxyanisole

Aliases:
BHA
INS 320
E320

Category:
Antioxidant

Function:
Used as an antioxidant food additive.

Description:
The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India identifies butylated hydroxyanisole (BHA) with International Numbering System (INS) number 320.

Regulatory Information:
India — FSSAI's Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations compendium, Version XXI dated 31 January 2022, lists BHA (INS 320) with category-specific maximum levels. Table 15.1 (snacks and savouries based on potato, cereal, flour or starch) lists 200 mg/kg and notes 15 and 130. This is the compendium's entry for that category, not a finding about the amount in any particular product or confirmation that no later amendment exists.

International scientific evaluation — JECFA's 1988 evaluation (33rd meeting) records an ADI of 0–0.5 mg/kg body weight per day. Its record also notes that 1998 intake estimates exceeded that ADI in three member states that supplied data, while mean estimates from seven other states did not; the high-consumer exceedance magnitude and duration could not be estimated from the available data.

European Union scientific evaluation — EFSA's 2011 re-evaluation established an ADI of 1.0 mg/kg body weight per day and reported refined intake estimates at current use levels were generally below that ADI. This is a scientific exposure assessment, not a product-specific use permission or amount measurement. EU legal conditions are maintained separately in the Commission's Union-list database.

Assessment Notes:
The evidence distinguishes India-specific permission conditions from JECFA and EFSA scientific intake evaluations. The product's BHA concentration is not available here. Neither an ADI nor a listed maximum alone proves this product complies or establishes its actual intake. Check the applicable regulation version, product category, notes, and measured level before making a compliance claim.
