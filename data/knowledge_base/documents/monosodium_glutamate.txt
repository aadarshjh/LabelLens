Ingredient:
Monosodium Glutamate

Aliases:
MSG
INS 621
E621
Sodium glutamate
Monosodium L-glutamate

Category:
Flavour Enhancer

Function:
Enhances savory and umami taste in foods and balances overall savory flavor profiles.

Description:
Sodium salt of the common amino acid L-glutamic acid, commercially manufactured by bacterial fermentation of carbohydrate sources such as sugar beet, sugar cane, or molasses using Corynebacterium glutamicum.

Regulatory Information:
US FDA classifies MSG as Generally Recognized as Safe (GRAS) under 21 CFR 182.1. JECFA allocated an Acceptable Daily Intake (ADI) of "not specified" in 1987. FSSAI approves its use under Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) in specified food categories per Food Safety and Standards Regulations.

Assessment Notes:
Extensive scientific evaluations by JECFA, FDA, and EFSA have found no causal relationship between MSG consumption at typical dietary levels and reported adverse symptoms in the general population.
