Ingredient:
Potassium Chloride

Aliases:
KCl
INS 508
E508
Potassium salt
Muriate of potash

Category:
Salt Substitute

Function:
Replaces sodium chloride to reduce sodium content while contributing salty flavor, and functions as a mineral nutrient, flavor enhancer, and firming agent.

Description:
Naturally occurring inorganic metal halide salt composed of potassium and chlorine, extracted from mineral deposits such as sylvite or carnallite and purified through crystallization.

Regulatory Information:
US FDA affirms potassium chloride as Generally Recognized as Safe (GRAS) under 21 CFR 184.1622 and permits the alternate name "potassium salt" on ingredient statements. JECFA evaluated potassium chloride in 1979 and assigned an ADI of "not limited". EFSA re-evaluated potassium chloride (E 508) in 2019 and concluded that it raises no safety concern at reported food additive uses.

Assessment Notes:
Provides bioavailable potassium, an essential dietary electrolyte. It imparts a salty taste profile that can carry bitter or metallic notes at higher sodium-substitution ratios.
