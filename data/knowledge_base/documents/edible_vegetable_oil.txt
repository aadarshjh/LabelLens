Ingredient:
Edible Vegetable Oil

Aliases:
Vegetable Oil
Edible Veg Oil
Veg Oil
Cooking Oil
Refined Vegetable Oil

Category:
Edible Vegetable Oil

Function:
Serves as a frying medium, cooking medium, lipid base, and textural component in processed foods and snacks, providing mouthfeel, crispness, and dietary energy.

Description:
Edible vegetable oil is a broad regulatory classification for food-grade refined, cold-pressed, or blended oils extracted from plant sources (such as oilseeds, legumes, grains, or fruit mesocarp). In food manufacturing and packaged snacks, the term denotes plant-derived lipids suitable for human consumption without designating a singular botanical species.

Regulatory Information:
In India, FSSAI regulates edible vegetable oils under Regulation 2.2 of the Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011, establishing quality, purity, acid value, and labeling parameters for single and blended edible vegetable oils. Codex Alimentarius Commission establishes international quality parameters under CXS 210-1999 (Standard for Named Vegetable Oils) and CXS 19-1981 (Standard for Edible Fats and Oils). In the United States, FDA regulations under 21 CFR 101.4(b)(14) govern vegetable oil ingredient declarations.

Assessment Notes:
Generic edible vegetable oil declarations are nonspecific as to the exact plant origin unless qualified by specific parenthetical oil declarations on the product packaging. Edible vegetable oils are standard food macro-ingredients composed of fatty acid triglycerides with no established Acceptable Daily Intake (ADI) limit.
