Ingredient:
Maltodextrin

Aliases:
Corn maltodextrin
Wheat maltodextrin
Tapioca maltodextrin
Dextrin maltose

Category:
Thickener/Bulking Agent

Function:
Provides viscosity, bulk, mouthfeel, and texture without high sweetness; serves as a spray-drying carrier, dispersing agent, and stabilizer in powdered and processed foods.

Description:
Nonsweet, nutritive saccharide polymer consisting of D-glucose units linked primarily by alpha-1,4 bonds, obtained by partial enzymatic or acid hydrolysis of edible plant starches with a Dextrose Equivalent (DE) of less than 20.

Regulatory Information:
US FDA affirms maltodextrin as Generally Recognized as Safe (GRAS) under 21 CFR 184.1444. EFSA recognizes maltodextrin as a standard food ingredient and evaluated its digestibility and energy contribution without safety concerns. FSSAI authorizes maltodextrin as a food additive and bulking agent under Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP).

Assessment Notes:
Rapidly broken down into glucose by alpha-amylase and mucosal enzymes in the human small intestine, exhibiting a high glycemic index comparable to pure glucose.
