Ingredient:
Whey Powder

Aliases:
Whey
Dried Whey
Sweet Whey Powder
Acid Whey Powder
Demineralized Whey Powder

Category:
Dairy Ingredient

Function:
Provides dairy solids, enhances texture, binds water, improves browning, and serves as a carrier for flavorings and seasonings.

Description:
Whey powder is obtained by spray drying liquid whey, the fluid co-product resulting from the coagulation of milk by rennet or acid during cheese or casein manufacturing. It consists primarily of lactose, whey proteins (beta-lactoglobulin and alpha-lactalbumin), and mineral salts.

Regulatory Information:
Affirmed as Generally Recognized as Safe (GRAS) by the US FDA under 21 CFR 184.1979. Regulated in India by FSSAI under Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011, Regulation 2.1.18. Governed internationally under Codex Alimentarius Standard CXS 289-1995.

Assessment Notes:
Contains milk protein and lactose; subject to mandatory declaration as a milk allergen in major regulatory jurisdictions. Consumed as a macronutrient food ingredient rather than an additive, with no numerical Acceptable Daily Intake (ADI) limit established under Good Manufacturing Practice.
