Ingredient:
Cheese Powder

Aliases:
Powdered Cheese
Dried Cheese
Dehydrated Cheese
Cheese Seasoning Base

Category:
Dairy Ingredient

Function:
Imparts savory cheese flavor, aroma, and color to snack coatings, seasonings, sauces, prepared mixes, and baked goods.

Description:
Cheese powder is produced by melting natural or processed cheese into a liquid slurry, often with the addition of water, emulsifying salts, and sometimes milk solids, and subsequently spray drying the mixture into a fine, free-flowing dry powder.

Regulatory Information:
In the US, regulated under FDA food standards in 21 CFR Part 133 (Cheeses and Related Cheese Products) and general food labeling provisions under 21 CFR Part 101. In India, regulated by FSSAI under the Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, with specific compositional criteria covering moisture (max 5.0%) and milk fat (min 40.0% on dry matter basis).

Assessment Notes:
Manufactured from dairy cheese and dairy solids; classified as a milk allergen requiring allergen declaration. It is consumed as a food ingredient/seasoning component rather than an additive, with no numerical ADI limit established.
