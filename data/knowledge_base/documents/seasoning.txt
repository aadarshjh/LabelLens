Ingredient:
Seasoning

Aliases:
Seasonings
Seasoning Mix
Seasoning Blend
Savory Seasoning

Category:
Seasoning / Flavouring Blend

Function:
Provides characteristic savory flavor profile, aroma, taste enhancement, and seasoning surface coating to snack foods, extruded products, and prepared dishes.

Description:
A formulated composite blend of spices, condiments, salts, hydrolyzed proteins, yeast extracts, sugars, and permitted flavoring agents applied to food products to impart specific flavor profiles (such as cheese, spicy, or tangy notes).

Regulatory Information:
In India, FSSAI regulates spices, condiments, and composite seasonings under Regulation 2.9 (Spices and Condiments) and the Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011. In the United States, FDA regulates seasonings and flavor blends under 21 CFR 101.22 (Spices, flavorings, colorings and chemical preservatives) and 21 CFR Part 182.

Assessment Notes:
Seasonings are composite ingredient preparations rather than single chemical substances. Individual sub-ingredients (such as salt, spices, flavor enhancers, or dairy solids) are typically declared in parenthetical sub-ingredient lists on product labels according to statutory labeling requirements.
