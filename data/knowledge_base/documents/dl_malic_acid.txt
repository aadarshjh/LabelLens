Ingredient:
DL-Malic Acid

Aliases:
Malic Acid
INS 296
E296
2-Hydroxybutanedioic acid
Hydroxysuccinic acid

Category:
Acidity Regulator

Function:
Malic acid is an acidity regulator. The JECFA record also assigns it the functional class acidity regulator and lists flavouring agent as a separate functional class.

Description:
JECFA identifies DL-malic acid as 2-hydroxybutanedioic acid (hydroxysuccinic acid), CAS 6915-15-7, INS 296. This identity applies to the DL form and should not be used to resolve a different malate salt without its own code or label evidence.

Regulatory Information:
India — FSSAI's Food Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations compendium, Version XXI dated 31 January 2022, lists DL-malic acid (INS 296) at GMP in several specified categories, including fruit juice and vegetable juice entries and fruit or vegetable nectars. The entry is category-scoped; it does not establish permission for every food. The inspected Table 15.1 entry for snacks and savouries based on potato, cereal, flour or starch does not list DL-malic acid. This historical compendium snapshot is not represented here as confirmation that no later amendment exists.

International scientific evaluation — JECFA evaluated DL-malic acid at its 13th meeting in 1969 and records an ADI of "not specified" within the group ADI for malic acid and its sodium, potassium and calcium salts. JECFA notes that the ADI does not apply to D(-)-malic acid and its salts for very young infants. An ADI is a toxicological intake value, not proof that a particular product complies with food-category permissions or is safe at an unknown level.

European Union — EFSA's 1 July 2024 call for data identifies malic acid as E 296 and states that its re-evaluation was ongoing; the call is not a final scientific opinion or an authorization amendment. The European Commission's Union-list database is the source for EU authorized uses and conditions. No final EFSA re-evaluation conclusion is asserted in this entry.

Assessment Notes:
The cited evidence verifies ingredient identity and additive code, provides historical FSSAI category and GMP conditions, and records JECFA's scientific evaluation. It does not establish use permission for an unverified product category or establish the amount used. Keep an assessment insufficient when the category, applicable regulation version, or use level needed for the conclusion is unknown.
