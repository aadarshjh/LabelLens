Ingredient:
Silicon Dioxide

Aliases:
INS 551
E551
Silica
Amorphous Silica
Synthetic Amorphous Silica

Category:
Anticaking Agent

Function:
Prevents clumping and caking, promotes powder flowability, adsorbs excess moisture, and serves as a carrier for flavors and active components in dry food mixtures.

Description:
Silicon dioxide is a chemical compound of silicon and oxygen (SiO2). For food use, it exists exclusively in synthetic amorphous (non-crystalline) form, produced via wet processes (precipitated silica or silica gel) or vapor thermal processes (pyrogenic/fumed silica).

Regulatory Information:
Approved by the US FDA under 21 CFR 172.480 as a direct food additive for anticaking use at levels not exceeding 2 percent by weight of the food. Evaluated by JECFA (INS 551) with a group ADI of "not specified". Authorized in the European Union as food additive E 551 under Regulation (EC) No 1333/2008, with EFSA re-evaluations confirming no safety concern across authorized uses.

Assessment Notes:
Synthetic amorphous silica does not possess crystalline quartz toxicity; it is biologically inert, insoluble in gastrointestinal fluids, and largely excreted unabsorbed. No safety concern identified at permitted regulatory levels.
