Ingredient:
Disodium 5'-Ribonucleotides

Aliases:
Disodium ribonucleotides
Disodium 5'-ribonucleotide
INS 635
E635
Sodium 5'-ribonucleotide

Category:
Flavour Enhancer

Function:
Enhances and intensifies umami and savory flavors synergistically when combined with monosodium glutamate, used in savory snacks, seasonings, and packaged noodles.

Description:
Equimolar mixture of disodium 5'-inosinate (IMP, INS 631) and disodium 5'-guanylate (GMP, INS 627), manufactured by enzymatic degradation of yeast ribonucleic acid (RNA) or microbial carbohydrate fermentation.

Regulatory Information:
JECFA evaluated disodium 5'-ribonucleotides in 1974 and re-evaluated them in 1993, assigning an ADI of "not specified". EFSA re-evaluated ribonucleotides in 2020 and confirmed that they present no safety concern at permitted uses. FSSAI authorizes INS 635 as a flavour enhancer under Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) in specified foods.

Assessment Notes:
Ribonucleotides are naturally occurring purine derivatives metabolized into uric acid in the human body. Individuals with clinical metabolic purine disorders such as hyperuricemia or gout typically manage dietary purine intake.
