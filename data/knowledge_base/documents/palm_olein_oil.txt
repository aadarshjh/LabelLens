Ingredient:
Palm Olein Oil

Aliases:
Palmolein Oil
Palm Olein
RBD Palm Olein
Refined Bleached Deodorized Palm Olein

Category:
Edible Vegetable Oil

Function:
Used as a frying oil, cooking oil, shortening ingredient, and confectionery fat, providing thermal stability, texture, and oxidative resistance.

Description:
Liquid fraction separated by thermal crystallization and fractionation of refined palm oil extracted from the fleshy mesocarp of the oil palm fruit (Elaeis guineensis).

Regulatory Information:
FSSAI establishes compositional and purity standards for Palmolein under Regulation 2.2.1(18) of the Food Safety and Standards Regulations, 2011. The Codex Alimentarius Commission (FAO/WHO) establishes international quality and identity standards under CXS 210-1999 (Standard for Named Vegetable Oils). US FDA regulates edible vegetable oils including palm olein as safe food substances under Title 21 CFR.

Assessment Notes:
Composed primarily of oleic acid (approx. 40-43%), palmitic acid (approx. 40-45%), and linoleic acid (approx. 10-12%). Liquid at ambient room temperature, it can cloud or form reversible crystals at cooler storage temperatures.
