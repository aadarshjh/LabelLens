Ingredient:
Lactic Acid

Aliases:
INS 270
E270
2-Hydroxypropanoic acid
DL-lactic acid
L-lactic acid

Category:
Acidity Regulator

Function:
Adjusts and buffers food acidity, inhibits bacterial spoilage, functions as a mild flavoring agent, and preserves dairy, fermented, and processed foods.

Description:
Organic alpha-hydroxy acid naturally present in sour milk products and fermented foods, commercially produced via bacterial fermentation of carbohydrates (glucose or sucrose) by Lactobacillus species or through chemical synthesis.

Regulatory Information:
US FDA affirms lactic acid as Generally Recognized as Safe (GRAS) under 21 CFR 184.1061. JECFA evaluated lactic acid and its salts and established an ADI of "not limited" / "not specified". FSSAI permits lactic acid under Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) in diverse food categories.

Assessment Notes:
L-lactic acid is an endogenous intermediate in human cellular carbohydrate metabolism. In infant foods, JECFA and EFSA note that D-lactic acid should be limited because infant metabolism processes the D-isomer more slowly.
